# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of CIGP-Protocol, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, particularly on technical and regulatory disagreements.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism, especially on cryptographic and statistical claims.
- Focusing on what is best for the integrity and credibility of the protocol.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting the guarantees CIGP provides (e.g. claiming CIGP certifies a casino as "fair" or "honest") in project communications or documentation.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer via the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile (`github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse`). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the general principles of the Contributor Covenant, rephrased for this project.
